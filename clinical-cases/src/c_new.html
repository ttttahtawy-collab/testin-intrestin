A 34-year-old woman on the combined oral contraceptive pill develops sudden pleuritic chest pain and breathlessness 3 days after a 12-hour flight. Her left calf is swollen and tender. Pulse 112/min, BP 118/74 mmHg, RR 26/min, SpO₂ 91% on air. She has no previous VTE, no cancer and no hemoptysis.
  1. Calculate her Wells score and interpret it (two-tier). [2]
  2. What is the next diagnostic test? When would a D-dimer be the right first test instead? [2]
  3. CTPA confirms PE. Echo shows RV/LV ratio 1.2 and troponin is raised. Classify the PE and give the treatment with doses. [3]
  4. Two hours later her BP falls to 82/50 mmHg. What do you do? [1]
  5. Special: How long should she be anticoagulated? Name one situation where warfarin is mandatory and one where LMWH is preferred. [2]

Case # Cardiology · 10 marks

A 76-year-old woman with hypertension and type 2 diabetes has had palpitations and mild breathlessness for "about a week". Pulse 138/min, irregularly irregular; BP 132/80 mmHg; she is alert with no chest pain. ECG: narrow QRS, no P waves, irregular RR intervals.
  1. What is the diagnosis? List 3 investigations to look for a cause. [2]
  2. Is she stable or unstable? Give the acute rate-control drug with dose. [2]
  3. Can she be cardioverted today? Explain. [2]
  4. Calculate her CHA₂DS₂-VASc score and state the anticoagulation decision. [2]
  5. Special: What would you do if she became hypotensive and confused? Which drug is dangerous in pre-excited AF (WPW)? [2]

1Atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response. Any 3: TSH (thyrotoxicosis), electrolytes, echo (valve disease, LA size, LV function), troponin, CBC, glucose, kidney function.1 + 1
2Stable (no hypotension, ischemic pain, ↓consciousness or pulmonary edema). Rate control: Metoprolol 2.5–5 mg IV or Diltiazem 0.25 mg/kg IV (long-term: β-blocker, non-DHP CCB or digoxin).1 + 1
3No. AF for >48 h or unknown duration → ≥3 weeks of anticoagulation or a TEE to exclude LA thrombus before cardioversion (risk of stroke). (If <48 h: rhythm control, e.g. amiodarone 300 mg over 1 h.)2
