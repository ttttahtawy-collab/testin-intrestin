  1. What is the diagnosis and the organism? [1]
  2. How does non-gonococcal urethritis differ (incubation, discharge, organisms, lab)? [2]
  3. Write the treatment with doses. Why is a second drug added? [3]
  4. List 2 complications in men and 2 in women. [2]
  5. Special: What is Reiter syndrome? Name one other essential step in management. [2]

QModel answerMarks
1Gonococcal urethritis: Neisseria gonorrhoeae (Gram-negative kidney-shaped diplococcus, oxidase +).1
2NGU: incubation 2–3 weeks; scanty mucoid "morning drop"; Chlamydia trachomatis D–K, Mycoplasma genitalium, Ureaplasma; ≥5 PMN/HPF without diplococci, NAAT.0.5 each
