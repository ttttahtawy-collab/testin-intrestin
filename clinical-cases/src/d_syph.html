Case # Venereology · 10 marks

A 25-year-old man has a single painless ulcer on the coronal sulcus that appeared 3 weeks after unprotected sex. The ulcer is firm and indurated with a clean base. He has bilateral rubbery, non-tender, non-suppurating inguinal nodes.
  1. What is the most likely diagnosis? Give 2 differential diagnoses and how each differs. [3]
  2. List the investigations and the role of each. [3]
  3. Write the treatment, including the option if he is allergic to penicillin. [2]
  4. Special: 6 hours after the injection he has fever, chills and tachycardia. What is happening, and what do you do? [1]
  5. How do you confirm cure? [1]
