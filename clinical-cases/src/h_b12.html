Case # Hematology · 10 marks

A 55-year-old woman with hypothyroidism has fatigue, a lemon-yellow tint and tingling in both feet. Examination: loss of vibration sense and joint-position sense in the legs, positive Romberg test. Hb 8 g/dL, MCV 115 fL, WBC and platelets mildly low, reticulocytes low, LDH raised.
  1. What is the most likely diagnosis and cause? Name the neurological complication. [2]
  2. List the investigations that confirm it, including one that distinguishes it from folate deficiency. [3]
  3. Write the treatment with doses for this patient. [3]
  4. Special: A junior doctor wants to start folic acid while the results are pending. Why is this dangerous? [2]
