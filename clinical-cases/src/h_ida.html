Case # Hematology · 10 marks

A 32-year-old woman (60 kg) with heavy periods complains of fatigue and craves ice. She has spoon-shaped nails and angular cheilitis. Hb 8 g/dL, MCV 68 fL, RBC 3.9 ×10¹²/L, RDW high; the smear shows pencil cells.
  1. Give 2 differential diagnoses of microcytic anemia and show how iron studies separate them. [3]
  2. Calculate the Mentzer index and interpret it. [1]
  3. Write her oral iron treatment (drug, dose, duration, how to take it) and how you check that she is responding. [3]
  4. Special: Give 2 indications for parenteral iron and calculate her total iron deficit (Ganzoni), target Hb 15 g/dL. [3]
