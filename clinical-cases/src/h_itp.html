Case # Hematology · 10 marks

A 24-year-old woman presents with petechiae on her legs, gum bleeding and epistaxis for 3 days. She is otherwise well and has no splenomegaly or lymphadenopathy. Platelets 8 ×10⁹/L (large platelets on the smear); Hb, WBC, PT and aPTT normal.
  1. What is the most likely diagnosis? Give 3 conditions you must exclude. [2]
  2. What do the bleeding time, PT and aPTT show, and how does this differ from hemophilia and DIC? [2]
  3. Write her emergency management with doses. [3]
  4. When would you transfuse platelets in this condition? [1]
  5. Special: She relapses on tapering steroids. List the second-line options. [2]
