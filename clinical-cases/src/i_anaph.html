Case # Immunology · 10 marks

Five minutes after an IM penicillin injection, a 30-year-old woman develops generalised flushing and hives, a hoarse voice with stridor, wheeze and abdominal cramps. BP 70/40 mmHg, pulse 135/min, weak.
  1. What is the diagnosis? Give 2 features that distinguish it from a vasovagal faint. [2]
  2. Write the immediate management in order, with doses and route. [4]
  3. Which blood test supports the diagnosis, and when should it be taken? [1]
  4. Special: She improves after 30 minutes and wants to go home. What do you tell her, and what does she leave with? [2]
  5. What is the immune mechanism? [1]
