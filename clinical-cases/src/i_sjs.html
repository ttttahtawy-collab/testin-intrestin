  1. What is the diagnosis? How is it classified by body-surface area? [2]
  2. Which hypersensitivity type is this? [1]
  3. Outline management. [4]
  4. After recovery, can a drug provocation test confirm the culprit? [1]
  5. Special: How would DRESS differ clinically from this patient? [2]
