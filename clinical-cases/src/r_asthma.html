Case # Chest · 10 marks

A 24-year-old woman with asthma, who uses only a salbutamol inhaler, arrives breathless. She can speak only single words, is using accessory muscles, RR 34/min, pulse 128/min, SpO₂ 88% on air, PEF 40% of predicted, loud wheeze.
  1. Classify the severity of this attack, giving 2 features that support it. [2]
  2. Write the immediate management in the ED with doses. [3]
  3. One hour later she is drowsy with a quiet chest. ABG: PaCO₂ 46 mmHg. Interpret this and state what you do next. [2]
  4. How is asthma confirmed on spirometry when she is stable? [1]
  5. Special: What is wrong with her current maintenance treatment, and what is the preferred GINA approach? [2]
