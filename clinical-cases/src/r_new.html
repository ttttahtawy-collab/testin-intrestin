A 68-year-old man (50 pack-years) with COPD has had 3 days of increased breathlessness and more, greener sputum. RR 28/min, SpO₂ 82% on air, widespread wheeze. In the ambulance he was given 15 L/min O₂ via a non-rebreather mask and is now drowsy. ABG: pH 7.29, PaCO₂ 68 mmHg, PaO₂ 95 mmHg.
  1. Interpret the ABG and explain why he became drowsy. [2]
  2. How should oxygen be given in this patient (device, %, target)? [1]
  3. Write the drug treatment of the exacerbation with doses and durations. [3]
  4. When is NIV indicated, and when does he need intubation? [2]

  2. How do you manage him? How would you manage a rim <2 cm with no symptoms? [2]
  3. How would management differ in a 65-year-old with COPD? [1]
  4. Special: A trauma patient has severe distress, hypotension, distended neck veins and tracheal deviation away from the silent side. What is it, and what do you do immediately (exact site)? [3]
  5. Give 3 indications for referral to thoracic surgery in pleural disease. [3]
