Case # Chest · 10 marks

A 35-year-old man (60 kg) has had a cough for 5 weeks with streaky hemoptysis, evening fever, night sweats and 6 kg weight loss. CXR: right upper-lobe cavity.
  1. List the investigations to confirm the diagnosis, saying what each offers. [3]
  2. Write his treatment regimen with doses for his weight. [3]
  3. Match the side effect to the drug: (a) orange urine, (b) red-green colour blindness, (c) acute gout, (d) burning feet. Which supplement prevents (d)? [2]
  4. Special: Define treatment failure and MDR-TB. [2]
