Case # Tropical / ID · 10 marks

A 40-year-old sheep farmer who eats home-made unpasteurised cheese has had 4 weeks of fever that comes and goes in waves, drenching sweats with a mouldy smell, low-back pain and a painful swollen right testis. There is mild hepatosplenomegaly.
  1. What is the diagnosis? Name the complication he has. [2]
  2. List the confirmatory investigations, including any special instruction to the lab. [2]
  3. Write the preferred regimen with doses and duration, plus an all-oral alternative. [3]
  4. MRI shows lumbar spondylitis. How does this change treatment? [1]
  5. Special: His pregnant wife is also infected. What do you give her and why? What is the main cause of death in brucellosis? [2]
