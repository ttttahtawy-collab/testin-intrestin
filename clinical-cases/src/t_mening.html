Case # Tropical / ID · 10 marks

A 19-year-old university-dorm student has 12 hours of fever, a severe headache and vomiting. Neck stiffness; a petechial rash that does not blanch with the glass test; GCS 14; no focal signs, no papilledema. BP 100/60 mmHg.
  1. What is the most likely diagnosis and organism? [1]
  2. What should happen in the first 30 minutes? When is a CT needed before LP? [3]
  3. Describe the expected CSF findings. [2]
  4. Give the empirical antibiotics and the adjunct, with doses and timing. [2]
  5. Special: His roommates are worried. What do you give them? [2]

1Acute bacterial meningitis with meningococcemia: Neisseria meningitidis.1
