He now has 10 days of fever and right upper quadrant pain referred to the right shoulder, with tender hepatomegaly and intercostal tenderness.   1. What is the most likely diagnosis? How did the organism reach the liver? [2]
  2. Compare it with a pyogenic abscess and a hydatid cyst (aspirate/imaging). [2]
  3. Write the drug treatment with doses. Why is a second drug needed? [3]
  4. When is drainage indicated? [2]
  5. Special: Which bloody-diarrhea organism must not be treated with antibiotics or loperamide, and why? [1]

  3. Define acute liver failure. Which finding would make you worried in this patient? [2]
  4. Name the oral drugs for chronic HBV with doses. [1]
  5. Special: His partner is 20 weeks pregnant. Which hepatitis virus carries a 20–25% mortality in pregnancy? Give the HBV vaccine schedule. [2]

A 16-year-old boy from a Nile Delta village who swims in irrigation canals complains of painful urination with blood in the last drops of urine. His 45-year-old uncle from the same village was admitted with hematemesis; he has splenomegaly and a shrunken-looking liver on ultrasound with "pipestem" periportal fibrosis.
  1. Name the species causing the boy's illness and the uncle's illness. [2]
  2. List 3 long-term complications of the urinary form. [1.5]
  3. Give the treatment with dose. Is it safe in pregnancy? [2]
  4. How would you manage the uncle's variceal bleeding (main lines)? [1.5]
  5. Special: A tourist develops fever, urticaria and eosinophilia 4 weeks after swimming in the Nile. What is this, and how is it treated? [3]

1Boy: S. haematobium (urinary). Uncle: S. mansoni (intestinal/hepatic: Symmers pipestem fibrosis → presinusoidal portal hypertension, varices).1 + 1
2Any 3: bladder calcification, ureteric obstruction/hydronephrosis, squamous cell carcinoma of the bladder, recurrent UTI, renal failure.0.5 each
3Praziquantel 40 mg/kg on one day (single dose or 2 doses 4–6 h apart). Yes, safe in pregnancy.1.5 + 0.5

  3. How do you differentiate it from CMV and toxoplasma mononucleosis (one clinical + one lab point each)? [2]
  4. Outline management. When are steroids indicated? [2]
  5. Special: What advice do you give about contact sports, and why? [2]
